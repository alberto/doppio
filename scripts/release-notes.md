A menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake: click the cup to toggle it, or let it switch on by itself while the apps you choose are running.

**Requirements:** macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon Mac.

### Installing

1. Download `Doppio-{{VERSION}}.zip`, unzip it and move **Doppio** to Applications.
2. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it can't check it for malicious software. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
   Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Doppio.app`
3. Doppio lives in the menu bar (no Dock icon). Right-click the cup for the menu and settings.
